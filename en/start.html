Getting started with Cyber

From the invite to a fully protected server, in 10 minutes. Click a command to copy it.

  1. 1

    Invite Cyber

    Click the button below, pick your server and confirm. You need the « Manage Server » permission. Community and Security work right away. Extra modules are added from the Public modules tab of the dashboard.

    Add Cyber to my server
  2. 2

    Move Cyber's role

    Discord only lets a bot manage the roles placed below its own. Go to Server Settings → Roles and drag the Cyber role right under your admin role.

    @Admin @Cyber ← here @Staff @Member
  3. 3

    Turn on the logs

    Create a private #logs channel for your staff. Cyber will post sanctions, deleted or edited messages, automod actions and closed tickets there.

  4. 4

    Greet newcomers

    A welcome message and a role given automatically when someone joins. Available variables: {membre} (member) {nom} (name) {serveur} (server) {nombre} (member count).

    CyberAPPToday at 2:02 PM
    New member

    Welcome @Enzo to My server! You are our 42nd member!

  5. 5

    Protect the server

    Turn on automatic moderation, then publish the verification panel in a channel like #verification. Accounts younger than 24 hours will be blocked.

  6. 6

    Open your support

    In a #support channel, publish the « Open a ticket » button. Each ticket is a private channel between the member and your team.

  7. 7

    Levels and rewards

    Members earn XP by chatting. Choose where level-ups are announced and which roles to give.

  8. 8

    The extras

    Temporary voice channels, self-assigned roles with buttons and a suggestions channel.

  9. ✓

    Check

    Show your whole setup, then discover every command with /help.

Need help? Join the support server: its guide channel walks you through each feature step by step. All of this can also be done in a few clicks from the dashboard, which has a short tutorial on your first visit.
