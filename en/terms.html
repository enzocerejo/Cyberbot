1. Overview

Cyber is a free, non-commercial Discord bot, developed and hosted on a personal basis by Enzo Cerejo ("the developer"). It offers moderation, levels, tickets, giveaways and fun features, an optional networking learning module (Academy), and an online dashboard to configure it.

By adding Cyber to a server, or by using its commands or its dashboard, you accept these terms and the privacy policy. If you do not accept them, do not use Cyber.

2. Who can use Cyber

You must be old enough to use Discord in your country (13 at the very least) and follow Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

3. Rules of use

You may not use Cyber to:

  • harass, threaten, discriminate against or harm other users;
  • send spam or get around the bot's limits (command cooldowns, AI quotas…);
  • try to disrupt, overload or exploit a flaw in the bot, the website, the dashboard or their hosting;
  • use the network commands (/pingip, /dns…) against systems without permission;

  • publish, through tags, embeds, reminders or giveaways, content that is illegal, hateful, sexual or that infringes the rights of others.
