The creator of Cyber

About me

I'm Enzo Cerejo, a French networking and cybersecurity student. I created Cyber on a whim, one evening in October 2026.

Who I am

I'm studying for a BTS CIEL, a French two-year technical degree in cybersecurity, IT, networks and electronics, with a focus on IT and networks. What I enjoy most is networking, security and infrastructure: setting things up, testing them and understanding how they hold together.

To learn more about my background, projects and skills, my portfolio is at enzocerejo.fr (in French).

An idea on a whim

Cyber was never planned. On 25 October 2026, I just wanted a bot of my own to try things out on a Discord server where I was an admin. The very first question was simple: "Can you help me make my own Discord bot?"

The idea grew fast. Most servers stack five or six bots: one for tickets, one for moderation, one for levels, one for giveaways… So I thought: why not one free bot that does it all? That became Cyber's goal.

How Cyber is made

Cyber is a personal project, free and ad-free. It is built with the help of an artificial intelligence (Claude) that writes the code. On my side, I decide what the bot should do, test every version on my servers, handle the infrastructure (domain name, DNS, hosting) and sign off on the security choices.

Every step is described in the project documentation (in French), including the problems I ran into and what I learned.

What matters to me

  • Security first: minimal permissions, no secret ever published, regular code audits.
  • Respect for your data: Cyber keeps only what it needs, and anyone can see or delete their data with /mydata.
  • For everyone: the bot, the website and the dashboard are in English and French.
  • Listening: most features come from members' ideas, and a server can even ask for a custom module.

Where Cyber stands

One week after it was born, Cyber was verified by Discord. It now brings together moderation, automod, tickets, levels, giveaways, polls, verification and much more, plus an optional module, the Academy, to study networking, born from my passion for my studies. The latest updates are in the changelog.
