v2.3November 15, 2026Latest

Polls

  • New polls with /poll: up to 10 choices, voting with buttons, live results with bars, and an automatic end (7 days by default, from 1 minute to 30 days).
  • Options: several choices per person, hidden results until the end, any channel. You can change or remove your vote, and the "My vote" button shows it privately.
  • Polls tab in the dashboard: create a poll with a preview, follow the votes, end a poll, choose which role can create them and whether the winner is announced.
  • Questions and choices written by members are no longer mistranslated in English channels.
v2.2November 14, 2026

Setup guide and tutorial

  • Interactive guide on the support server: 10 topics (inviting Cyber, language, moderation, tickets…), step by step, in French or English, with a button that opens the right dashboard tab directly.
  • Dashboard tutorial: on your first visit, a guided tour has you make the real settings (language, log channel…). Every step can be skipped, and the "Tutorial" button starts it again.
  • Your setup: a list in the Overview shows what is left to configure. It disappears once everything is done, or when you hide it.
  • Longer custom module codes: 16 letters and digits.
  • /language: one language at a time (French or English), or "Automatic" to follow the server and the channel.
  • Website fully in English: every page has its English version (cyberbot.fr/en/), and your FR / EN choice is kept from page to page.
v2.1November 14, 2026

Feedback, images and bilingual panels

  • New Feedback tab: an opinion, an idea or a bug, with up to 3 images. The message goes straight to the Cyber team. Logged in to the dashboard? Your Discord account is added automatically.
  • Images in module requests: on the website, in the dashboard and in /requestmodule. Hidden photo information (GPS location…) is removed.
  • Ticket panel in French, English or both: choose in the dashboard before sending. "Both" adds an "English" button that translates the panel in place for everyone, with your own English version if you want.
v2.0November 14, 2026

Custom modules

  • Custom modules are open: a feature coded just for your server, installed with a unique code (CYB-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX) in Public modules → "Module code", or with /modules code:.
  • The code only works on the server it was created for; 5 tries every 10 minutes, then a pause.
  • A custom module's commands only show up on the servers where it is installed, with their own section in /help.
  • Request a module from the website, the dashboard (Public modules) or /requestmodule: the request goes straight to the team.
  • Cyber's code reorganized (by area) and website redesigned with line icons.
v1.9.1October 30, 2026

Bilingual channels

  • Bilingual channels: in the chosen channels or categories, Cyber's automatic messages have an English / Français button that switches the message's language in place, without sending a new one (/config language with the bilingual option, or the dashboard, General tab).
  • Dashboard in English: EN / FR button at the top right.
  • A server's "/" menu only shows the commands of the installed modules.
  • Dashboard: the chosen sub-section lights up even at the very bottom of the page.
v1.9October 30, 2026

Modules, languages and advanced rules

  • English command names for everyone (/help, /learn, /config…), with their description in French if your Discord is in French.
  • Language per channel: French, English, or both, with a list of channels and categories where Cyber writes in English (/config language or the dashboard, General tab).
  • Community and Security are active right away. The Academy becomes an optional module, made out of passion, to install from Public modules.
  • Public modules redone: installed modules on one side, search on the other (most installed, best rated, new, by name or by ID), ratings from 1 to 5.
  • Dashboard menu with the sub-sections of each tab to jump straight there, and a collapsible Modules section at the top.
  • More detailed "Choose a server" page (members, online, boosts, language, modules) and boosts in the overview.
  • Rules: Discohook-style advanced mode with several colored cards, titles, small text, separators and images.
v1.8October 29, 2026

Cyber speaks English

  • Cyber is now bilingual French / English: replies, buttons, forms and automatic messages.
  • Server language with /config language or in the dashboard (General tab), and a personal language for each member with /language.
  • Commands also have their English name in Discord's "/" menu (/help, /learn, /challenge…).
  • Verification: an "Unverified" role given to each newcomer then removed once validated, roles created or chosen from the dashboard, channels hidden from unverified members in one click.

v1.7October 29, 2026

A more complete dashboard

  • Dashboard: switch tabs without losing your settings, then Reset or Save in one click, just like on Discord.
  • New tabs: Community, Security, Academy and Public modules.
  • Automod: banned word library (insults, hate, harassment, scams…) that recognizes variants (c0nn4rd, c.o.n.n.a.r.d…), plus a list of allowed words.
  • Tickets: 123 ready-to-use templates (recruitment, complaint, partnership, shop, games, roleplay…), editable, with questions when a ticket is opened.
  • "Join to Create" temporary voice channels: creator channel in one click, default options, and a panel to rename, limit, lock, hide, kick… (/voice).
  • Daily challenge: a private quiz every day in an automatically created channel, with a leaderboard posted every day.
  • All of Cyber's messages move to Discord's new format (Components V2).
