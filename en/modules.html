Custom modules

A feature that no bot has? It gets coded just for your server, then added to Cyber by typing a simple code, like inserting a cassette.

  1. 1

    You describe your idea

    Below or on the support server, explain what your server needs: a mini-game, an economy system, a command made for your community, a link with another service…

  2. 2

    The module is coded for you

    We agree together on what it should do, then it is built and tested. It follows the same rules as the rest of Cyber: no useless permission and no data kept without a reason.

  3. 3

    You get your code

    A unique code, reserved for your server, like CYB-7F3A-K9QM-2XRT-B4WZ. It does not work anywhere else.

  4. ✓

    You add it in one click

    In the dashboard, open Public modules (at the very top of the menu), then the small « Module code » button, and paste your code: Cyber installs the module by itself, with no restart and no new bot to invite.

    MODULE · CYB-7F3A-K9QM-2XRT-B4WZ

Just for your server. The code only works on the server it was made for, and an admin can uninstall the module at any time in Public modules (or with /modules). To get started, describe your idea below.
